**Anki_Card_Life_Engine_Algorithm**
1. Card Entry to System guided by rule of {cardProgress}
- {cardProgress} based on Variables not limited to;
	+ Some Factor of {reviewSuccess} from {reviewStep #} to {reviewStep #} for {totalCards} adjusted for {factor}
	+ Observed Streak of {login&Review}
	+ Observed Velocity of {cardEntry} given Observed Streak of {login&Review}
- Approach is to start with assumed generic {recallSlope} and Fit to {cardProgress}
- Each Card Reviewed by [Instructor] 

2. Review of Card after 1 Minute {reviewStep 1} (Assumption: Time)
	If reviewPass:
		2a. Review of Card after 10 Minutes (Assumption: Time)
	If reviewFail:
		2b. Review of Card after 1 Minute (Assumption: Time)

3. If 2a; Review of Card after 1 Day (== {intervalCurrent}) (Assumption: Time)
	If reviewPass:
		3a. Review of Card after {intervalCurrent} * 2.4 (== {factor}) (Assumption: Time)
	If reviewFail:
		3b. Review of Card after {intervalCurrent}
			if reviewPass:
				then 3a.
			elif reviewFail:
				then Review of Card after {previousIntervalCurrent}

4. If 3a; Review of Card after {intervalCurrent} == {previousIntervalCurrent} * {factor} (Assumption: Time)
	+ (rest is recursive as Step 3.)

**VariablesAdjustable in Anki_Card_Life Per User**
+ list of all applicable {intervalCurrent} (Can Increase or Decrease per Step)
+ the {factor} (Can Increase or Decrease)
+ {cardEntry} Velocity (Can Throttle or Allow for More)
